About Us

Meet Peter Noutaros

Peter Noutaros has been running Norwest Fresh Seafood since 2002 — over 20 years of passion for bringing the freshest, highest quality seafood to the Hills District of Sydney.

Every morning Peter personally selects the finest seafood from Sydney's markets to ensure every customer gets nothing but the best. From whole fish to premium gourmet platters, Peter's commitment to quality has earned Norwest Fresh Seafood the 2022 Sydney Hills Local Business Award for Outstanding Fresh Food.
